ABOUT
GZIM SINANI

I am Gzim Sinani. Photography always has been the thing I am the most passionate about, but I started my career as a professional photographer in 2014. Through all these years of work I enjoyed every moment behind the camera, and I always have tried to show the best side of the moment as I felt it. In this website you will get to see these special moments, different views of people and nature. You will get to see happy people in different places, marriages, vacations, student at their prom night, people enjoying the nature, my collaborations with some of the most famous artists in Kosovo and of course some of the most beautiful landscapes all the way through Kosovo,
